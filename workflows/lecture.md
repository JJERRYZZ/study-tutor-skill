# Complete lecture workflow
1. Inspect the supplied deck and verify its total page/slide count. If only partial extraction is available, state the limitation.
2. Build a **coverage ledger** with every slide/page or contiguous range, main topic, visual/formula/code flags, and status: pending / explained / transition-only / unreadable. Do not infer topic labels without examining content.
3. Group slides by natural topic boundaries, not equal-sized arbitrary ranges. Provide a compact roadmap; do not let the roadmap replace teaching.
4. Explain in original slide order. For each substantive slide or tightly related range: **what it says → what it means → why it matters → worked example/diagram walkthrough where useful → course relevance**. Mention actual slide numbers. Describe chart axes, legends, arrow direction, table schema, figure labels and notation when meaningful.
5. Explain all substantive material, not just selected highlights. Title, recap and near-duplicate slides may be covered briefly and explicitly.
6. For long decks, stop at a coherent boundary with: **Completed: pages X–Y; Remaining: pages Z–N**. Do not compress the remaining pages into a few bullets or imply the lecture is complete.
7. Once all pages are covered, synthesize a concept hierarchy and connections to earlier weeks if available; optionally give a short high-priority recap.
8. Run `validation.md` checks. No unverified claims about exact slide counts or coverage.

**Prohibited shortcuts:** summarize a 78-page deck as five themes when full teaching was requested; skip a figure-only page; describe unread material as completed; make up page ranges; produce only a quick review instead of full explanation.
