# After-class review
Target 3–5 minutes of reading. Do not reuse full-lecture length.
- Core Ideas: typically 5–10 high-value concepts, adjusted to lecture density.
- Must Understand: key causal/mathematical logic.
- Must Remember: definitions, formulas, assumptions and English keywords.
- Easy to Confuse: precise contrasts and traps.
- Quick Check: normally 2–3 high-yield questions, with short answers; fewer if content is simple.
Clearly separate instructor emphasis from model-derived importance. Link to earlier weeks only when those materials are available.
