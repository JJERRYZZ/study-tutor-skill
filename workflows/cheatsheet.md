# One-sheet, double-sided cheatsheet
1. Confirm course scope and whether notes are permitted in the actual assessment; if unknown, make it a **study cheatsheet**, not a claim that it is exam-authorized.
2. Rank content using instructor emphasis, official scope, tutorial/quiz frequency, central formulas, misconceptions and problem-solving utility.
3. **Tier 1:** key formulas + symbols + applicability conditions, hard-to-recall definitions, traps, decision rules, repeated question types.
4. **Tier 2:** compact algorithms, comparisons, tiny worked examples when space permits.
5. **Tier 3 remove:** background stories, repeated prose, unimportant examples, easy material.
6. Organize **Front** and **Back**, preferably A4 unless user/course requires another format. Chinese assistance + original English terms. Compact tables, arrows, short labels; no huge titles or decorative color fills.
7. Never solve overflow by making text unreadably tiny. Cut Tier 3, then Tier 2. Keep all necessary conditions attached to formulas.
8. For generated PDF, inspect actual rendered pages and confirm **exactly two pages** (front and back), no clipping/overlap, legible font and correct equations. If not checked, say not verified.
